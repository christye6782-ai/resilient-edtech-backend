Act as an experienced Computer Vision Engineer,Create a professional and fantastic application for teachers in rural areas to plan a lesson that use technology, using the best resources albeit the constraints they are facing.


Agent 1: The Analyst
= Teacher will upload own lesson plan
- Agent will analyse if lesson plan is according to DSKP, scheme of work and topics in the textbook

Agent 2: The Auditor
- Teacher will choose constraints faced at school especially in terms of technology integration
- For example: Limited connectivity, no internet, 1 device only, 1 screen etc.
- Agent will propose the best way to carry on with lesson plan by proposing ways to overcome constraints, at the same time keep the use of technology
- Teacher can print lesson plan


Agent 3: FAQ
-Agent can actually explain terminology based on lesson suggested and also ways to execute them
-For example: If agent 2 suggested the use of 'plickers', Agent 3 will answer what 'plickers' is


Input:
1.Teacher upload own lesson plan
2. teacher choose constraint/s

output:
1. revised lesson with best integration of technology based on constrained but still aligned with dskp, textbook and scheme of work
