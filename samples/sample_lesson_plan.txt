Lesson Plan
Subject: Science
Form: Form 1
Topic: Cell as a Unit of Life

Learning Objective:
By the end of the lesson, students will be able to identify the general structure
of animal and plant cells and state the function of each cell structure.

Set Induction (10 min):
Teacher shows a picture of an onion and a piece of human skin and asks what they
are made of.

Development (40 min):
1. Teacher explains the parts of a cell using the textbook diagram (Chapter 2).
2. Students draw and label an animal cell and a plant cell.
3. Students discuss in groups the function of the nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane,
   cell wall and chloroplast.

Closure (10 min):
Students answer five short questions to recap.

Assessment:
Worksheet with labelling and short-answer questions.

Materials:
Textbook, whiteboard, worksheet.
